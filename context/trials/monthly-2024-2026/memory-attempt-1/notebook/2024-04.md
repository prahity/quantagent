Rule 1: Entry Trigger
- Enter long only if all three conditions met at order date (open):
  1. close > prior 5-day high
  2. 3-day cumulative return >= 0.5% (from signal bar close)
  3. breakout magnitude >= 0.2% above prior 5-day high
  4. **10-day cumulative return > 0%** (trend filter; added per P3 evidence)
- Evidence P2 (2024-03-01 to 2024-03-31): Trigger activated for MSFT and SPY, profiting +2.30% and +2.77%. AAPL triggered Rule 1 (3-day return 0.637% >= 0.5%) but fell -4.49% by period end; 0.5% return filter alone insufficient without trend confirmation. *Supported: trigger works for strong trends (MSFT/SPY), insufficient alone.*
- Evidence P3 (2024-04-01 to 2024-04-30): Agent held cash until 04-12, then bought AAPL and MSFT triggers; both declined sharply (AAPL -31.3% from trigger, MSFT -8.17%, SPY -4.17% buy-and-hold). 10-day returns were negative; filter would have prevented entries. *Contradicts rule without 10-day filter; filter added to avoid churn in falling markets.*
- Mark unproven: optimal 0.5% vs 1% threshold; exact 10-day return cutoff (test >0% vs >-1%).

Rule 2: Position Sizing & Caps
- Initial allocation: 12% of equity per entry.
- Max per position: 25% of equity.
- Max total deployed equity: 50%; remaining 50% cash buffer for new entries and drawdown protection.
- Evidence P2 (2024-03-01 to 2024-03-31): Agent used ~15% initial entries (MSFT ~15.37%, SPY ~15.37%, AAPL 13.87%). Total deployed never exceeded 50%, allowing cash buffer. AAPL loss partly due to full allocation into a reversing name; reducing initial from ~15% to 12% frees cash for outperforming names (MSFT, SPY) and provides scaling buffer. *Supported: 12% initial + 25% max permits scaling without overconcentration across P2 and P3.*
- Evidence P1 (2024-02-01 to 2024-02-29): With 20% initial and 25% cap, MSFT hit cap after initial fills; no scaling possible as MSFT rose +2.94%, SPY entry constrained by MSFT's near-25% occupancy. 15% initial would leave room for one scaling tranche while keeping <=25% per position. *Partial support: 15% initial works but 12% is more robust for capital efficiency (P3 evidence).*
- Mark unproven: 50% total deployed optimality vs 60% across longer histories.
