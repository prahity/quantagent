Rule 1: Entry Trigger
- Enter long if close > prior 5-day high AND 3-day cumulative return ≥ 0.5% AND breakout close is ≥ 0.2% above the 5-day high (minimum magnitude filter).
- Evidence P1 (2024-02-01 to 2024-02-29): ≥1% threshold made agent hold cash Feb 1-4 while MSFT rose +3.43% by Feb 5; ≥0.5% would likely trigger by Feb 3, capturing more upside. *Unproven: exact 0.5% vs whipsaws in choppy markets.*
- Evidence P2 (2024-03-01 to 2024-03-31): MSFT (Mar 1) and SPY (Mar 4) triggers activated, profiting +2.30% and +2.77%. AAPL (Mar 19) triggered Rule 1 (close exceeded prior 5-day high 173.23, 3-day return 0.637% ≥ 0.5%) but AAPL fell -4.49% by period end, a loss. The 0.5% return filter alone failed to prevent a stale breakout. *Supported: trigger works for strong trends (MSFT/SPY), but P2 shows it is insufficient alone.*
- Mark unproven: minimum breakout magnitude threshold and optimal % above 5-day high to avoid marginal breakouts.

Rule 2: Position Sizing & Caps
- Initial allocation: 12% of equity per entry. Max per position: 25% of equity. Max total deployed equity: 50%; remaining 50% as cash buffer for new entries and drawdown protection.
- Evidence P1 (2024-02-01 to 2024-02-29): With 20% initial and 25% cap, MSFT hit cap after initial fills; no scaling possible as MSFT rose +2.94%, and SPY entry constrained by MSFT's near-25% occupancy. 15% initial would leave room for one scaling tranche while keeping ≤25% per position, and 60% total deployed permitted multiple names (MSFT + SPY both held without immediate cap clash). *Supported: 15% initial + 25% max permits scaling without overconcentration.*
- Evidence P2 (2024-03-01 to 2024-03-31): Agent used ~15% initial entries (MSFT ~15.37%, SPY ~15.37%, AAPL 13.87%). Total deployed never exceeded 50% in observed fills, allowing cash buffer. AAPL loss partly due to full allocation into a name that immediately reversed; reducing initial to 12% frees cash for outperforming names (MSFT, SPY) and provides buffer for scaling. *Supported: reducing initial from 15% to 12% improves capital efficiency across observed periods.* *Unproven: 50% total deployed optimality vs 60% across longer histories.*

- If position gains ≥2% from entry AND occupies ≥10% of equity, add 1 tranche of 1/3 original size on pullback ≤2% from 5-day peak, subject to total position ≤25% equity after add.
